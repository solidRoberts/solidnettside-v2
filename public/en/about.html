About us

About Solid Marketing

Solid Marketing is a local agency in Fjerdingby, Rælingen. We help trade and service businesses in Oslo and Akershus get more enquiries, with ads on Facebook, Instagram and Google and with websites built to win customers. We work in Norwegian and English.
