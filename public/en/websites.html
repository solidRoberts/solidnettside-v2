Questions and answers about websites

Here are answers to what people most often wonder about before getting a new website.

What does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages you need, which features to include (for example a form, price list or articles) and whether we write the copy and provide the images. After a free meeting you get a written price before we start, so you know what you are paying for.

How long does it take to build a website?

It mostly depends on the scope and how quickly we receive copy, images and feedback from you. You get a timeline together with the quote.

Can I update the website myself?

Our websites are built to be fast and secure, without a heavy content management system. We make changes and updates for you. What is included in ongoing hosting is stated in the quote.

Can my website be in both Norwegian and English?

Yes. We can build the website in Norwegian, in English, or in both with a language switcher – just like this one.

Will a new website make me visible on Google?

We build the website with basic search engine optimisation: clear titles and descriptions, fast pages, proper structure and a sitemap that helps Google find the pages. Nobody can promise a specific ranking on Google, and neither do we.

Do I need a website when I have a Facebook page?

A Facebook page is a nice addition, but you don’t own it, and it rarely shows up when people search for a service on Google. A website on your own domain gives you a place you control. That is where customers find your services and contact details, and can get in touch.

Do you build websites for businesses other than tradespeople?

Yes. We build websites for all kinds of businesses, but know trade and service businesses especially well.

What do you need from me?

Your logo if you have one, information about your services, and ideally your own photos of yourself, your vans and the jobs you do. Real photos build more trust than stock photos. We help you with the rest.
