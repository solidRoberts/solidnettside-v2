Project: website

A new website for Ingas Elegance AS

Ingas Elegance AS is Inga’s salon at Stallbakken 5C in Rælingen, offering gel nails, nail design, manicure, pedicure and waxing. We built the new website.

Project facts

Client
Ingas Elegance AS
Industry
nail design, manicure, pedicure and waxing
Location
Rælingen
Delivery
new website
Website
www.ingaselegance.no (opens in a new tab)

The brief

The goal was a website that shows the treatments and the nails Inga creates, and makes it easy to call or send a text message to book an appointment.

What we delivered

  • Responsive design that works on phones and computers
  • An overview of the treatments: gel nails, manicure, pedicure and waxing
  • A gallery of nails from the salon, which can be opened in a larger size
  • A section about Inga, with her training and course certificates
  • Buttons to call and send a text message, also fixed at the bottom on phones
  • The address with a link to a map

How we worked

We created three design directions for Ingas Elegance to choose from. We then built out the chosen direction with the salon’s own photos.
