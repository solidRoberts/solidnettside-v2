Ads

Ads for tradespeople on Facebook, Instagram and Google

We create and run Facebook ads for tradespeople in Oslo and Akershus – for plumbers, electricians, painters, carpenters and other trade businesses. The ads also appear on Instagram, and we set up ads on Google. The goal is enquiries from people in your area, and you can always see what the budget is spent on.

What we do for you

We handle everything, from the plan and the ads to follow-up and reporting.

  • A plan for the ads

    We make a plan for your trade and your area, based on what works for tradespeople where you operate. No ready-made templates.

  • We create the ads

    Copy, images and carousels that stand out when people scroll, and that make it easy to get in touch.

  • Enquiries straight to you

    The ads are built to bring enquiries from people who need the job done, not just clicks and likes.

  • Tracking who gets in touch

    We set up tracking properly, so we can see which ads bring enquiries, spend the budget where it works and show ads again to people who have visited your website.

  • Weekly adjustments

    We review the ads every week, keep what works and change what doesn’t.

  • A simple monthly report

    How many people got in touch, what each enquiry cost, and what you got for your money.

Google Ads

Ads when people search on Google

Google ads are shown to people who are already searching for your service, for example “plumber Lillestrøm”. Ads on Facebook and Instagram are shown to people in your area while they scroll, often before they have started looking.

We set up the Google ads and follow them up. You can choose Google, Facebook and Instagram, or a combination, and we help you work out what suits your business.

Questions and answers about ads

Wondering about something? Here are the questions we get most often.

Do Facebook ads work for tradespeople?

Often, but nobody can promise results in advance. With Facebook and Instagram you reach people in your own area before they start looking for a tradesperson. How well it works depends on your trade, area, offer and budget. That is why we test several variants and adjust continuously, and you can always see what is happening.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need and how big your ad budget is. We sit down with you for a free meeting with no obligation, get to know your business, and give you a concrete proposal tailored to your situation. You always know what you are paying for before you decide.

How much should I spend on ads?

There is no single right number. It depends on your area, your trade and how many new jobs you can actually take on. You decide the budget yourself, and we help you find a level that makes sense.

How quickly will I see results?

It varies from business to business, and depends on things like your trade, budget and competition in your area. We don’t promise overnight miracles – but we are honest with you about expectations along the way and adjust continuously to get the most out of every krone.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. We believe good results should be the reason you stay – not a contract. We want a partnership that lasts because it works, not because you are locked in.

I have never advertised before – is this for me?

Absolutely. We take care of the technical side and explain everything in a way that is easy to understand. You don’t need to know anything about advertising beforehand.

The ads are in Norwegian – do I need to speak Norwegian?

No. We write the ads in Norwegian for your customers and talk to you in English. You approve everything before it goes live, and we explain what each ad says.

Do I need a website to advertise?

No. The ads can collect enquiries through a form directly in Facebook or Instagram. But many people check your website before they get in touch, so a good website makes it easier to build trust. We can build that too. Read about the websites we build.

What is the difference between Facebook ads and Google ads?

Google ads are shown to people who are already searching for a service. Facebook and Instagram ads are shown to people in your area while they scroll, often before they have started looking. We work with both, and help you work out what suits your business.
