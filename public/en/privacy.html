Privacy policy

How Solid Marketing (Media Gruppen Murnieks) processes personal data when you contact us. Last updated 26 September 2026. If this English version differs from the Norwegian one, the Norwegian version applies.

Data controller

Media Gruppen Murnieks (Solid Marketing), org. no. 937 471 467, Opsalvegen 94, 2008 Fjerdingby, Norway. Contact: Roberts@solidmarketing.no.

What we collect, and why

When you send the form on the contact page, we receive your name, phone number and/or email address, and anything else you choose to write in the form, together with the address of the page the form was sent from. When you call or email us, we receive what you provide yourself.

The purpose is to respond to your enquiry and arrange a meeting. The legal basis is legitimate interest and steps taken prior to entering into a possible agreement (GDPR Article 6(1)(f) and (b)).

Data processors

We use these services to receive and respond to enquiries:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) forwards the form to us as an email. The service is based in the USA, so the data may be transferred to the USA.
  • Google Workspace is our email provider.
  • Vercel hosts the website and may log technical data such as IP addresses in server logs.

How long we keep the data

Enquiries are deleted once they have been handled, and no later than 12 months if no agreement is made.

Cookies and tracking

The website uses no cookies and no tracking. The ad calculator stores and sends nothing.

Your rights

You have the right to access the data we hold about you, and to ask for it to be corrected or deleted. You can also object to the processing. Send an email to Roberts@solidmarketing.no, and we will reply as quickly as we can.

If you believe we are processing personal data in breach of the rules, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet (opens in a new tab).
