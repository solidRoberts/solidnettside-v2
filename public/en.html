Questions and answers

Wondering about something? Here are the questions we get most often.

What does Solid Marketing do?

We help trade and service businesses get more enquiries. We do this with ads on Facebook, Instagram and Google, and with websites that turn visitors into customers. You can choose one or both.

Can we work with you in English?

Yes. Meetings, emails and reports can all be in English. The ads and the website itself are usually in Norwegian, since that is what your customers search in, and we write those for you.

Do you build websites too?

Yes. We build websites for all kinds of businesses, but know trade and service businesses especially well. The website can stand alone or work together with the ads, so people who click land on a page built to make them get in touch. Read more about the websites we build.

Where are you based?

We are based in Fjerdingby in Rælingen and take on work across Oslo and Akershus, including Lillestrøm, Lørenskog and the rest of Romerike.

What does it cost to work with you?

It depends on whether you need ads, a website or both, and on the scope. After a free meeting with no obligation, you get a concrete proposal. You always know what you are paying for before you decide.

How do I get started?

Book a free 30-minute strategy meeting, or call us directly. We reply the same working day. Book a meeting or find our phone numbers.
